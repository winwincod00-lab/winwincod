## Implemented isolated components

`src/integrity-v1/domain.ts` uses bigint scaled to 30 decimal places, accepts exact decimal strings and rejects numeric(65,30) overflow. It does not round input or use floating money. Frozen cost snapshots retain null unknown costs with evidence/source; projected, delivery-recorded and settlement-verified reporting are explicit. Signed quantity recognition/reversal is bounded by line quantity. Raw margin calculations do not post money and do not replace the existing clamped runtime profit formula.

Wallet transitions enforce balance >= held >= 0 and available = balance - held. Whole holds are consumed or released once. Stock transitions track available, reserved, in transit, returned pending inspection and damaged quantities. RESTOCK is an inspected movement into available, not a second stock bucket counted again. Reconciliation requires carrier-account-scoped identities, known expected amounts and exact MAD arithmetic; duplicate/partial/missing identities are ambiguous, never invented matches.

`payment-contract.ts:proposedPayBatch` is an injected transaction contract with no database adapter. It requires a signed policy reference, current seller, dual operation identity and immutable receipts; locks wallet then sorted stock resources and rereads batch/lines; preserves explicit Admin approval, one order per customer and five eligible units. Exact reviewed line costs plus existing 20 MAD/order are charged with reservations, journal, history and receipt in ONE Serializable transaction. A mock rollback/retry is not PostgreSQL atomicity.

`proposals/integrity-v1/schema.proposed.prisma` copies the current model and adds explicitly related `fi_*` models. It uses isolated URL names and is outside Prisma migration discovery. The proposed SQL adds composite tenant FKs, unique identities, exact money constraints, deferred aggregate checks, immutable facts, private internal kernels, legacy writer fences and a restricted role contract. There is no simulated runtime ledger, seeded money or automatically generated historical data.

## Data and transaction design

**COGS:** one `FiOrderLine` binds one legacy order/SKU/quantity; immutable versioned snapshots separately store seller charge basis and supplier purchase basis, source, evidence and capture operation. UNKNOWN remains null. Evidence review appends a version and never overwrites an earlier snapshot or existing recorded profit. A paid line charge pins the actual chosen snapshot and fee; a price edit cannot change it. Quantity recognition records partial delivered/returned quantities and compensating references, rather than mutating past recognition. Returns do not automatically reverse fees, create credit, or convert damaged inventory to sellable inventory.

**Wallet:** each wallet has an evidence-backed opening, balance and held projection; ledger accounts have documented opening debit balances. A journal has at least two postings summing to zero. Cash events must equal wallet posting effects, and projection must equal opening plus events. Hold events affect available funds, not cash or profit. Operation keys are unique per seller/kind/request AND seller/kind/business identity; changed-payload retries fail. Bank funding proof has a unique source-account/external identity, separate from an HTTP key. A withdrawal snapshots the payout destination opaquely and holds funds at request time. Prepare an external payout intent, commit, then perform an authorized external action outside the DB transaction; unknown/in-flight outcomes retain the hold. Consume only against confirmed evidence; no timeout-based release. No bank transport is implemented here.

**Stock:** payment reserves available stock under locks in the same transaction as debit. Dispatch consumes reserved into transit only with paid approved orders, existing manual dispatch authorization and a valid database warehouse grant. Delivery exits transit; a carrier return observation alone changes no physical stock. Physical receipt moves to quarantine; inspection and disposition cap quantities against received units; RESTOCK consumes unused sellable disposition. Adjustments need grant, actor and evidence, and cannot make a bucket negative. Product balances equal opening plus movements; reserved balances equal open reservations. The proposed release kernel supports full canceled orders; partial paid cancellation remains blocked pending explicit workflow and refund policy.

**Settlement:** carrier account -> shipment reference -> immutable expected COD terms -> carrier statement/line -> active match -> bank remittance allocation -> explicitly approved wallet entitlement -> journal. Store raw observation provenance without assuming LIVCASH endpoints, webhook guarantees, identifier uniqueness or live availability. V1 matching is one-to-one; partial/multiple ambiguous lines require review. Partial shipment COD requires explicit agreed terms, never an invented pro-rata allocation. Correction appends revocation/new facts and reverses any prior posted effect before replacing an active match. A statement match is not bank payment; wallet credit requires verified funding/approved entitlement. No settlement imports, bank proofs or payments are fabricated.

**Security and boundaries:** authenticated actor comes from the trusted server, not seller-supplied IDs or arbitrary DB session variables. Composite FKs carry seller identity through money, line and shipment references. New objects revoke PUBLIC/default grants. Proposed NOLOGIN owner and app roles must be distinct, without inherited ownership, bypass-RLS or schema CREATE. The internal functions are not publicly granted business APIs. Actual scoped RPC/read adapters and every route's integration are NOT IMPLEMENTED. Superuser/owner access remains an operational trust boundary. Legacy activated balances/stock are frozen rather than dual-written; adapters must read the new projections.

PostgreSQL CHECK constraints cannot establish cross-row invariants alone; the proposal therefore includes deferred constraint triggers and lock-based kernels. These still require real commit/concurrency verification. SECURITY DEFINER functions require a controlled search path and privilege review. See [PostgreSQL constraints](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/current/ddl-constraints.html) and [CREATE FUNCTION security](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/current/sql-createfunction.html).

## Owner decisions required before activation

No default approval is seeded. `FiPolicyDecision` records signed terms/effective scope; revocation prevents future operations without rewriting history.

1. Capture seller quote at creation or submission? What evidence determines supplier valuation (standard, actual lot or another method)? No historical price inference/FIFO is authorized.
2. When is profit recognized: delivery record or verified settlement? How do the existing 20 MAD batch charge and delivery commission relate in accounting? Preserve current formulas and disabled legacy 30 MAD path until signed clarification; do not silently remove, add or double-collect fees.
3. Return fees/refunds, damage losses, partial delivery COD and recognition reversal rules. Raw margin is not realized net profit. Partial legacy terminal state transitions need explicit design approval.
4. Withdrawal hold timing, independent payout approval, encrypted destination retention, evidence of paid versus approved, unknown bank outcomes and reconciliation ownership. No partial payout or automatic hold expiry is authorized.
5. Payment-time reservation, expiry, cancellation/refund order and insufficient funds during reversal. No negative wallet/debt policy is assumed. Inspection/adjustment roles, separation of duties and evidence retention need approval.
6. Settlement fee attribution, remittance allocation, duplicate/conflicting external evidence, negative statements, corrections and debt recovery. Reversals that cannot safely consume available funds must stop for owner resolution.
7. Legacy opening attestations, reconciliation tolerances (none assumed), record/PII retention and migration/cutover responsibility.
